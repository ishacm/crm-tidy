how it works

What happens to your export

  1. Read any exportHubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive or a spreadsheet, with English or German column names.
  2. Clean it upFixes name casing, checks emails, standardises countries, phone numbers and company names, and maps job titles to personas like finance or founder.
  3. Merge duplicatesThe same person with a different email casing, or listed twice at the same company, becomes one record. Every merge is logged.
  4. Fill the gapsA free check of each company's website first, then Apollo (capped so a free plan can't be emptied by accident), then Clay through a simple CSV round trip.
  5. Score and routeEach record gets a 0 to 100 fit score against your ICP, with reasons, and the best ones go to the right owner in Slack.

what you get

A CRM your team can actually use

  • A clean CSV, ready to import into HubSpot, sorted by fit
  • A before and after report: how complete each field was, and what got fixed
  • An ICP drafted from your website, as a short file you review and edit
  • Every new inbound lead scored in under a second, with a short brief for its owner

The demo uses fictional people at fictional .example companies. Real Apollo responses were checked against the code, and that check is now part of the tests.

try it in 60 seconds

No account, no API key, no cost

git clone https://github.com/ishacm/crm-tidy && cd crm-tidy
pip install -r requirements.txt
CRMTIDY_LLM=mock python -m crm_tidy.run --demo

Then open the report it prints. To run it on a real company, add a free Groq key: see the README.
